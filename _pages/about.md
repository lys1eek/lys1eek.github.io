##  Personal Information

My research interests include machine learning, stochastic optimization, and the theoretical foundations of deep learning and large language models (LLMs). I will be supervised by [**Prof. Difan Zou**](https://difanzou.github.io/) at the University of Hong Kong (**HKU**) and [**Prof. Junwei Su**](https://jwsu825.github.io/) at USTC.
